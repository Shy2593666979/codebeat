Composition: one centered symbol on a square canvas, using roughly 80% of the canvas, balanced center of mass. Absolutely NO white/light-blue rounded-square tile, NO badge, NO backing plate, NO background color, NO mockup, NO presentation sheet, NO checkerboard drawn into the artwork.
