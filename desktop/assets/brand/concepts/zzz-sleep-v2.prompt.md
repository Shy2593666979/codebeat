## Exact prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE polished original logo symbol for CodeBeat, a Windows coding productivity app associated with sleeping while coding tasks continue.

Essential correction: exactly THREE custom lowercase z glyphs, ordered SMALL, MEDIUM, LARGE from bottom-left to top-right. The leftmost lowest z must clearly be the smallest, the middle z medium, the rightmost highest z the largest. Approximately height ratios 0.40 : 0.65 : 1.00. Each letter reads unmistakably as lowercase z. This is the conventional increasing-size sleeping zzz gesture. No reversed size order.

Redesign the lettering itself with sophisticated custom geometric typography: bold and compact lowercase z, short balanced horizontal bars, a clean broad diagonal, softly rounded outside corners with precise tidy inner corners, gently softened flat-cut terminals. Harmonious proportions shared across the three sizes, optically adjusted thickness so the small z stays legible. More refined than an ordinary italic font. Upright letter skeletons with only a slight forward inclination, NOT a strong diagonal rotation. Elegant restrained simplicity, coherent visual rhythm, generous but not disconnected spacing, comfortable negative space, no overlapping letters. Avoid pill-shaped stacked strokes and overlapping ribbon folds. Avoid chunky cartoon bubble letters. No white lines running through the glyphs.

Palette: app primary blue #0078FF and cobalt #165DFF with a subtle cyan #19C9EE transition. Very restrained smooth flat gradient across the trio. The letterfaces should remain largely solid rich blue for strong recognition at small sizes. Crisp clean vector-like contours and uniform anti-aliasing; zero texture, zero speckles, no glow, no shadows, no embossing, no gloss or 3D extrusion. No moons, stars, brackets, clouds or other extra symbols. No wordmark, no CODE text, only the three lowercase z shapes.

Composition: a single compact balanced diagonal constellation in a square canvas, group centered with comfortable equal transparent margins, use about 78 percent of the canvas. Completely transparent background and gaps: NO backing tile, white plate, rounded square, backdrop, checkerboard, border, watermark or mockup. Output the finished isolated logo only.
